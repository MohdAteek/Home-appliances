# Sultan Home Appliances — Premium Kitchen Cooking Appliances Showroom

A modern, high-performance web showroom and ecommerce showcase dedicated exclusively to kitchen cooking appliances. Authorized dealer platform featuring **Siemens, Bosch, Faber, Elica, Gilma, Häfele, Crompton, and Hindware**.

---

## 🍳 Features & Specialized Modules

1. **Hero Showcase**: High-impact banner with live product metrics, fast search, category quick-jump, and interactive featured cooking appliance preview.
2. **Featured Brands Showcase**: Dedicated authorized dealer section for Siemens, Bosch, Faber, Elica, Gilma, Häfele, Crompton, and Hindware with manufacturer warranties and certifications.
3. **9 Cooking Appliance Categories**:
   - Built-in Gas Hobs
   - Smart Kitchen Chimneys & Hoods
   - Tabletop Gas Stoves & Ranges
   - Built-in Convection & Steam Ovens
   - Induction & Radiant Cooktops
   - Heavy-Duty Gas Burners & Wok Stations
   - Electric & Pressure Multi-Cookers
   - Kitchen Exhaust & Ventilation Blowers
   - Cooking Accessories & Care
4. **Master Appliance Catalog & Gallery Hub**: Filterable by 9 categories, 8 global brands, technology styles, price sorting, keyword search, technical spec modal, and instant WhatsApp inquiry.
5. **Why Choose Us Section**: Highlights 100% Genuine Authorized Products, Free Countertop Cutout Sizing Guidance, Certified Next-Day Installation, and Best Price Guarantee / 0% EMI.
6. **Promotional Offers & Festive Deals**: Curated Hob + Chimney + Oven packages with up to 40% OFF and free certified installation.
7. **Interactive Kitchen Appliance Setup Visualizer Studio**: Live 3D simulation of black glass hobs, quartz slabs, and circadian task lighting with 1-click JPG export.
8. **Cooking Package & Quotation Generator**: Interactive kitchen package estimator with itemized breakdown, warranty, and downloadable formal `.txt` quotation.
9. **Before & After Cooking Transformation Slider**: Interactive comparison slider showing traditional stoves upgraded to modern built-in hobs and filterless chimneys.
10. **3-Step Appliance Buying Guide Diagnostic Quiz**: Visual diagnostic assessing cooking intensity, kitchen layout, and tech priorities with downloadable recommendation lookbook.
11. **Appliance & Finish Moodboard Canvas Studio**: Interactive canvas to pin hobs, chimneys, ovens, and marble finishes with 1-click JPG composite export.
12. **Customer Testimonials**: Verified homeowner, chef, and modular kitchen architect reviews.
13. **Saved Appliances Drawer & Quote Booking Modal**: Persistent wishlist and formal appointment booking engine.
14. **Direct Contact Integration**: Call `8076224170` and direct WhatsApp integration.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Framework**: Angular 19 (Standalone Components, Signals)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS + Custom Metallic/Flame Design System
- **Icons & Typography**: SVG Icons, Outfit, Space Grotesk, Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Storage & State**: Angular Signals + LocalStorage client-side persistence
- **Export Engines**: HTML5 Canvas composite image generator + Text Blob exporter
